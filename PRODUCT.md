# Shopify Lead Intelligence

## Register
product

## Platform
web

## Users
A Shopify development agency researching businesses in the US, UK, Canada, and Australia. Researchers need to identify real opportunities, inspect evidence, and approve eligible prospects.

## Product Purpose
Automatically discover active Shopify stores, run technical audits, prioritize supported development opportunities, and produce downloadable reports. Outreach always requires human approval.

## Positioning
A continuously updated pipeline of Shopify prospects with traceable audit evidence.

## Brand Personality
Professional, clear, restrained. The product is a research workspace with an approachable Scout identity.

## Anti-references
Generic landing pages, fabricated findings, unsupported revenue claims, and opaque AI scores.

## Design Principles
Evidence precedes recommendations. Unknown data remains unknown. Dense research stays legible. Every automated run exposes its status and failures. Commercial outreach remains under human control.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
Semantic controls, visible keyboard focus, text with status colors, reduced motion support, responsive layouts. Accessibility is verified during implementation; no certification is claimed.
